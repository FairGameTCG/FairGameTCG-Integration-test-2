Who We Are

Built by Collectors, For Collectors

Fair Game TCG was started because we were tired of inflated prices, shady sellers, and cards that turned out to be fakes. We believe every trainer deserves a trustworthy place to buy, sell, and trade.

Based in Calgary, we specialize exclusively in Pokémon TCG - singles, booster boxes, sealed product, and high-value rare cards. Everything we sell is authenticated, accurately graded, and priced to be fair.
